What if I make another purchase?

Try one synthetic transaction without editing the JSON. This evaluates the same prior history independently on EACH fictional card, not your actual wallet allocation.

The new purchase may reduce other transactions' rewards under shared caps. Annual fee changes assume a complete calendar year. No actual issuer terms, merchant offers or bank-linked data are provided.
