# Code of Conduct

We want CredoRewards to be a constructive, technically rigorous and accessible open-source project.

## Our expectations

Contributors and maintainers should:

- Treat other participants with respect regardless of background, experience level or differing technical views.
- Give specific, actionable feedback about code, tests and financial assumptions rather than attacking people.
- Use constructive language and make space for new contributors to ask questions.
- Keep discussions focused on documented behavior, reproducible evidence and the safety of users' financial data.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property and the relevant licenses of upstream data and code.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of private information and coercive behavior are not tolerated.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact **support@credonomics.in** privately. Include the repository link and relevant public discussion references, but do not share sensitive personal information unnecessarily. Maintainers will review reports in confidence to the extent practicable and may moderate or remove inappropriate content, close threads or restrict participation.

For **security vulnerabilities**, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead; do not publish exploit details or financial data in an issue.

This code of conduct applies to the project's issue discussions, pull requests, code reviews and other official collaboration spaces.
